General electrical & fault finding

When something trips and won't reset, guessing gets expensive. We test circuit by circuit until we find the actual fault, then quote the repair before doing it.

  • Earth leakage and trip switch faults traced to the offending circuit
  • DB board upgrades, rewires and proper circuit labelling
  • Plug points, lighting, geyser elements and thermostats
  • Surge protection and prepaid meter faults
  • Storm damage and water ingress repairs on outdoor boxes

Backup power & solar installations

We size the system to what you actually run, not to the biggest kit on the shelf. You get a load calculation, a written quote and a realistic runtime estimate before anything is ordered.

  • Inverter and lithium battery installations, wall or floor mounted
  • Rooftop and ground-mount solar panel arrays
  • Essential-loads sub-board so the right circuits stay live
  • Generator changeover switches, manual or automatic
  • System monitoring set up on your phone before we leave

Commercial & industrial electrical

Offices, warehouses, workshops and body corporates. We work around your operating hours and leave you a maintenance record you can hand to an insurer.

  • Three-phase power distribution and sub-metering
  • Heavy-duty breakers, isolators and motor control gear
  • Machine wiring, cable trays and containment
  • Routine safety audits and thermal checks on distribution boards
  • Emergency and evacuation lighting compliance

Certificates of Compliance

Selling a property, renewing insurance, or taking transfer? A COC is a legal requirement and has to be issued by a registered person after a proper inspection. We inspect, list what needs attention, quote the remedial work — and only then issue the certificate.

  • Property transfer inspections your conveyancer will accept
  • Certificates for landlords, rentals and body corporates
  • Earth leakage trip times, insulation resistance and polarity recorded
  • Re-inspection after any alteration to the installation
